Research

My research focuses on statistical and deep learning approaches to solving problems in spatial, urban, and network data science. Several current projects include modeling the impact of law enforcement drug seizures on overdose, using machine learning techniques to study police use of force data, assessing the impact of gunshot detection technology on reducing gun violence, and modeling viral processes in coupled online-offline systems.
